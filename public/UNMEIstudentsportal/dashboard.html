UNMEI Student Portal & Mobile App

Portal Usage Agreement

Please read this Agreement carefully. It sets out the terms under which your Student Account, the Student Portal, and the UNMEI Learning mobile application operate. Acceptance is required before account features are activated.

This Portal Usage Agreement explains how your Unmei Nihongo Center Student Account works across the web portal and the UNMEI Learning mobile application. Please read it before accepting below.

1. Your Account

Your account was created from your enrollment details and stays connected to your registered course. It is for your personal use only, so please keep your password private and let the office know if anything looks unusual with your sign-ins.

2. Course Access

Course materials, schedules, and the mobile app become available once your payment has been verified by the administration. Your enrolled level determines the lessons, sections, and schedules shown to you.

3. Payments and Fees

You may settle tuition through bank transfer, GCash, other accredited channels, or over-the-counter at the school office. Submitted payments are marked pending until reviewed by the administration, and installment due dates appear on your Payment page. The registration fee is non-refundable.

4. Portal and Mobile App

The portal and the mobile app share the same student record. Practice activities completed in the app sync to your dashboard so your instructors can follow your progress. Syncing happens online at regular intervals, so recently finished work may take a few minutes to appear.

5. Academic Honesty

Attend your scheduled classes, do your own work, and use the portal respectfully. Quiz and assessment results recorded here form part of your class standing. If you have concerns about any record, coordinate with your instructor or the office.

6. Learning Materials

Lessons, quizzes, recordings, and other resources remain the property of Unmei Nihongo Center. They are for your personal study only, so please do not copy, share, or post them elsewhere without permission.

7. Your Privacy

The portal keeps only what your classes need: your name, contact details, enrollment and payment records, and learning progress. These are stored securely and seen only by staff and instructors involved in your course. They are never sold or shared outside the school. You may ask the office for a copy of your records or request corrections at any time.

8. Service Availability

We work to keep the portal reliable and will announce maintenance ahead of time whenever possible. Like any online service, brief interruptions can happen â€” for example during power or internet outages â€” and these are outside the school's control.

9. Account Status

Accounts may be paused for unsettled balances or misuse of the platform, and long-inactive accounts may be retired. If your account is affected, contact the office to settle requirements or request copies of your records before it is removed.

10. Withdrawing from a Program

If you need to withdraw, simply inform the school office in writing. Refunds of prepaid tuition follow the school's refund policy, and a completion certificate is released to students who finish their program in good standing.

11. Updates and Contact

This agreement may be updated as features or school policies change, and the portal will show you the latest version when that happens. Continuing to use the portal means you accept the current terms. For questions, visit the front desk during office hours, Mondays to Saturdays, or email admin@unmei-ph.com.
